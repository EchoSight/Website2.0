Meet Jack Curry

Experienced bat ecologist and workflow specialist.

Hi, I'm Jack Curry – an experienced bat ecologist and evidence-focused consultant based in Newbury. With over a decade’s experience in project delivery alongside protected species survey work, I specialise in delivering high-sensitivity bat surveys and robust, traceable reporting for ecological consultancies.

I’m also the developer of EchoSight Tracker, our integrated review platform that pairs thermal footage with acoustic datasets so teams can interrogate every data point with confidence.

My background combines protected species survey work, digital data collection, project and operations management, and workflow design. By focusing on workflow improvement and identifying the best tools for the job, I’ve developed specialist bat survey methodologies that pair hands-on expertise with powerful analysis tools – ensuring no bats are missed and every result is traceable, audit-ready, and easy for clients to use.

  • Bat survey specialist: Advanced thermal and acoustic techniques tailored to complex sites.
  • Evidence-based delivery: Transparent, reproducible reporting with defensible data trails.
  • Consultancy partner: We support your team without competing for clients.
