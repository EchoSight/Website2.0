Great crested newt (GCN): assistant-level support & DLL coordination

EchoSight provides HSI assessments, eDNA sampling coordination, and assistant-level field support alongside licensed ecologists, with District Level Licensing (DLL) documentation prepared to planning standards.

Book a call View pricing

Scope note: EchoSight does not hold a GCN survey licence. We deliver assistant-level support and coordinate licensable methods with partner ecologists who are licence holders.

Timelines & coordination

Traditional surveys: mid-March to mid-June. eDNA sampling: mid-April to end-June.
