Survey windows, coordination, and readiness

Mobilise assistant surveyors in line with Natural England guidance and your licence conditions.

Presence/absence season

Traditional methods typically span six visits between mid-March and mid-June. We support scheduling contingencies for weather and livestock access.

Population monitoring

Extended hours or multiple ponds can be staffed with experienced assistants familiar with DLL and mitigation monitoring requirements.

Mobilisation timeline

Availability is confirmed within one working day. Mobilisation packs (RAMS sign-off, kit lists, contact tree) are agreed ahead of night one.
