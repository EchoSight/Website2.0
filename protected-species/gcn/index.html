Focused on dependable GCN survey delivery

From mobilisation to nightly handover, we support your licenced ecologist with structured, professional field teams.

Mobilisation & compliance

We align with your RAMS, licence conditions, and site protocols before the first bottle trap is set.

  • Briefing packs covering ponds, access, and hazards
  • Biosecurity procedures and equipment sign-off
  • Flexible scheduling to react to weather and livestock

Experienced assistant surveyors

CSCS-qualified teams undertake bottle trapping, torching, and egg searching with licenced oversight.

  • Efficient trap deployment, checks, and removals
  • Live updates to your supervising ecologist
  • Safe handling and welfare monitoring every visit

Reporting & programme coordination

Datasets and nightly notes are collated ready to drop into your reporting templates and licence returns.

  • Completed trap logs, weather records, and sketches
  • Shared folders for photos and GPS-tagged evidence
  • Debriefs to agree next steps after every shift

Where we operate

Teams mobilise from Newbury with around 60 road miles of included coverage (120 miles of return travel), reaching Banbury, Cirencester, Guildford, Portsmouth, and similar destinations across Berkshire, Oxfordshire, Wiltshire, Hampshire, and neighbouring counties. Programmes further afield can be scheduled with travel allowances agreed in advance.

Travel within roughly 60 road miles of Newbury is included in guide rates (up to 120 miles of return travel). Mileage beyond this coverage is charged at £0.70 per mile, with accommodation passed through at cost where required.

We regularly support multi-pond networks, DLL commitments, and mitigation monitoring—ask about staging survey nights to align with your licenced ecologist’s availability.

Up to ~60 road miles from Newbury
