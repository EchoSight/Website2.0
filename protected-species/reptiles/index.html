Reptile survey types we deliver

Plain-English descriptions, so you know what you’re booking and why it matters.

Presence / Likely Absence

When to choose: Early stage scoping or to inform mitigation need.

What we do: Place and bed-in artificial refugia, then complete daytime checks under suitable weather, with visual encounter transects across suitable habitat.

You get: Geo-tagged records, effort/weather logs, and a clear “present / likely absent” conclusion for your site parcels.

Population Class Assessment

When to choose: Presence confirmed and you need relative population grading to scope mitigation.

What we do: A structured multi-visit programme (typically 6 visits) recording effort, temperature, wind, and encounter counts.

You get: Consistent-effort data and a relative class (low / medium / high) with maps and tagged imagery.

Translocation Support

When to choose: Licensed ecologist has set the strategy and you need reliable field delivery.

What we do: Daily checks of artificial refugia and directed hand searches, humane capture, temporary holding as instructed, and incident/welfare logging.

You get: Time-stamped check records, daily progress notes, and consumables/stock logs to feed into the licence file.

Hibernacula Inspections

When to choose: To inform mitigation and soft-strip timing or to verify potential over-wintering features.

What we do: Hand searching of log/compost piles and potential hibernacula, endoscope checks where appropriate, and georeferenced notes.

You get: An annotated inspection map, stills/footage catalogue, and practical follow-up recommendations.
