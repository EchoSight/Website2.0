Survey windows, methodologies, and reporting

Align survey effort with weather triggers while keeping stakeholders informed.

Survey windows

Checks are scheduled April–September, targeting 9–18°C conditions to evidence basking and dispersal behaviour.

Methodology

Programmed refugia inspections are paired with directed searches and spotlighting, delivered by licenced partners under EchoSight coordination.

Reporting

Outputs include mitigation phasing, GIS figures, and risk assessments issued within seven working days of the final visit.
