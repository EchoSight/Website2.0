Reptile surveys with practical mitigation support.

EchoSight delivers presence/likely absence and mitigation programmes for common and protected reptile species, integrating survey effort with your construction timelines.

Book a call View pricing

Species covered & methodologies

Surveys tailored to the presence of slow-worm, common lizard, grass snake, adder, and sand lizard.

Presence / likely absence

  • Target note and habitat suitability checks
  • Artificial refugia deployment with repeat checks
  • Spotlighting and directed searches where appropriate

Mitigation & translocation

  • Capture and relocation strategy design
  • Temporary and permanent receptor site assessment
  • Toolbox talks and H&S briefings for site teams

Deliverables & outputs

Clear reporting and mapping ready for planning and site management.

Reporting

Survey report with constraints, results, and mitigation recommendations aligned to industry guidance.

GIS outputs

GeoPackage, shapefiles, or layered PDFs showing survey effort, refugia locations, and receptor sites.

Health & safety

Method statements and risk assessments covering manual handling, sharp objects, and thermal stress.

Survey windows & scheduling

Reptile surveys typically run April–September with optimal conditions between 9–18°C.

We coordinate survey checks to align with weather patterns, ensuring coverage of early spring emergence and late season activity before hibernation. Reporting is issued within 7 working days of the final visit.
