Designed around dependable field delivery

We take the structure proven on our GCN survey programmes and apply it to reptiles: clear mobilisation, prepared survey teams, and quick turnaround of the site data you need for client reporting.

Mobilisation & logistics

We review your scope, confirm habitat coverage, and prepare health & safety documentation so survey days start smoothly.

  • Surveyor scheduling and land access liaison
  • Daily RAMS updates and briefing packs
  • Equipment drop, collection, and welfare checks

Experienced field teams

CSCS and first-aid trained ecologists undertake refugia checks, spotlighting, and transects in line with your method statements.

  • Lead ecologist with assistant support
  • Geo-referenced imagery and temperature logging
  • Live progress updates every survey day

Reporting handover

Notes, weather data, and capture records are compiled within 48 hours, ready to slot into your written reports and mitigation plans.

  • Spreadsheet-ready datasets and photo logs
  • Optional GIS shapefiles for habitat parcels
  • Direct liaison with your reporting ecologists

Coverage built for Newbury & surrounding counties

Teams are based from our Newbury hub and routinely deliver within a 60 mile radius, covering Berkshire, Oxfordshire, Wiltshire, Hampshire, and the surrounding counties. Priority schemes outside this catchment can be scheduled with overnight allowances.

Travel within 60 miles of Newbury is included in guide rates. Further mileage is charged at £0.55 per mile, with accommodation passed through at cost where needed.

Talk to us about phased developments or multi-site programmes—we will build a timetable that keeps your fieldwork efficient and compliant.

Survey types we support day-to-day

Presence / Likely Absence

Evening and dawn refugia checks combined with encounter transects to confirm baseline presence across suitable habitat parcels.

Population Class Assessments

Structured multi-visit programmes capturing effort, weather, and abundance data for robust relative population grading.

Translocation support

Daily pitfall trap checks, holding area monitoring, and welfare reporting to support licenced ecologists.

Hibernacula inspections

Winter checks of potential hibernacula, log piles, and refuges to inform mitigation and soft-strip plans.
