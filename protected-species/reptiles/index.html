Survey windows, coordination, and readiness

Deploy assistant surveyors in step with Natural England guidance and your mitigation milestones.

Survey windows

Checks are scheduled April–September, targeting 9–18°C triggers to evidence basking and dispersal behaviour without over-stressing animals.

Coordination

We align daily schedules, refugia rotations, and toolbox talks with your licenced ecologist and site management.

Readiness

Mobilisation packs confirm RAMS sign-off, kit lists, and comms trees ahead of the first visit, with updates shared after each check.
