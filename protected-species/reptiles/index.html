Survey windows & scheduling

Reptile surveys typically run April–September with optimal conditions between 9–18°C.

We coordinate survey checks to align with weather patterns, ensuring coverage of early spring emergence and late season activity before hibernation. Reporting is issued within 7 working days of the final visit.
