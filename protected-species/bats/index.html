Mobilisation & scope confirmation

Before stepping on site we validate constraints, stakeholders, and health & safety requirements.

  • Desk-based review of drawings, trigger lists, and roost history
  • Access liaison and RAMS aligned to your company procedures
  • Survey programme mapped to planning and licencing milestones

Experienced field methodologies

Licenced ecologists deploy calibrated detectors, thermal cameras, and manual survey effort to collect defensible evidence.

  • Dusk, dawn, and transect coverage tailored to site complexity
  • Thermal imaging capture with live call verification
  • Biosecurity, welfare, and noise controls embedded in every shift

Reporting & licence support

Data is processed in-house so you receive concise reports, mapping, and appendices ready for submission.

  • 5–7 working day turnaround on written outputs
  • Geo-referenced imagery, call analysis, and activity summaries
  • Advice on mitigation triggers and licensing pathways

Where we operate

Our bat survey teams mobilise from Newbury with around 60 road miles of included coverage (120 miles of return travel), reaching Banbury, Cirencester, Guildford, Portsmouth, and similar destinations across Berkshire, Oxfordshire, Wiltshire, Hampshire, and adjoining counties. We schedule further afield assignments with transparent travel allowances.

Travel within roughly 60 road miles of Newbury is included in guide rates (up to 120 miles of return travel). Additional mileage is charged at £0.70 per mile with accommodation passed through at cost when required.

Talk to us about phased developments, multi-night programmes, or rapid mobilisation around weather windows—we will build a timetable that keeps your evidence gathering compliant.

Survey types we support day-to-day

Choose the mix of inspections, activity surveys, and analysis your project requires.

Preliminary roost assessments

Daytime inspections of structures and trees to classify roost potential and trigger further effort.

Emergence & re-entry surveys

Licenced dusk and dawn coverage with thermal imaging and live call verification for robust evidence.

Activity transects & static monitoring

Transects, automated detectors, and remote review to map commuting routes and foraging hotspots.

Night-time bat walkover

Standard nocturnal walkover (5 km transect) covering sunset to post-emergence with live data capture and reporting.

Licence applications & mitigation

Method statements, compensation design, and monitoring plans produced alongside your lead ecologist.
