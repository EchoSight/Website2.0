Bat surveys grounded in evidence and licenced expertise.

From preliminary assessments to emergence surveys, EchoSight combines advanced thermal imaging, acoustic analysis, and licenced ecologists to support planning and licence submissions.

Book a call View pricing

Services delivered

Full lifecycle support for projects affecting roosts and commuting corridors.

Survey types

  • Preliminary Ecological Appraisal (PEA) and scoping assessments
  • Dusk and dawn activity surveys with real-time thermal imaging
  • Emergence and re-entry surveys tailored to building or tree roosts
  • Internal and external roost inspections with licenced ecologists
  • Remote static detectors (Batlogger, Anabat, Song Meter) for baseline monitoring
  • Natural England licence application and mitigation support

Deliverables

  • Methods and results aligned to BS 42020 and BS 42021
  • High-resolution mapping of activity and roost features
  • Risk assessment with clear recommendations and triggers
  • Mitigation and compensation outline ready for planning conditions
  • Appendices with call analysis, imagery, and raw data summaries

Survey windows, timelines, and equipment

Plan ahead to secure optimal data collection periods.

Survey windows

Activity surveys typically run from May to September, with building inspections possible year-round. We schedule visits to capture key seasonal triggers including maternity, swarming, and hibernation transitions.

Equipment & data handling

We deploy Pixfra A625P thermal cameras, Batlogger M2 detectors, and calibrated microphones. All recordings are processed using BatExplorer and Kaleidoscope, with QA reviews before delivery.

Timelines

Fieldwork is followed by reporting within 5–7 working days. Licence support documentation is produced in collaboration with your team to align with submission deadlines.
