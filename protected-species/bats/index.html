Survey windows, timelines, and equipment

Plan ahead to secure optimal data collection periods.

Survey windows

Activity surveys typically run from May to September, with building inspections possible year-round. We schedule visits to capture key seasonal triggers including maternity, swarming, and hibernation transitions.

Equipment & data handling

We deploy Pixfra A625P thermal cameras, Batlogger M2 detectors, and calibrated microphones. All recordings are processed using BatExplorer and Kaleidoscope, with QA reviews before delivery.

Timelines

Fieldwork is followed by reporting within 5–7 working days. Licence support documentation is produced in collaboration with your team to align with submission deadlines.
