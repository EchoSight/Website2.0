Survey windows, timelines, and equipment

Plan ahead to secure optimal data collection periods.

Survey windows

Activity surveys typically run from May to September, with building inspections possible year‑round. We schedule visits to capture key seasonal triggers including maternity, swarming, and hibernation transitions.

Equipment & data handling

We deploy Pixfra A625P thermal cameras, Batlogger M2 detectors, and calibrated microphones. Recordings are processed using BatExplorer and Kaleidoscope, with QA reviews before delivery.

Timelines

Fieldwork is followed by reporting within 5–7 working days. Licence documentation is produced collaboratively to align with submission deadlines.

Bat survey FAQs

Straight answers for planners, designers, and PMs.

When can bat surveys be carried out?

Activity surveys are typically May–September. Preliminary roost assessments and building inspections can be completed year‑round; we use activity windows where required to evidence likely impacts.

Do I need a licence?

A licence is required if works risk disturbing bats or damaging/destroying a roost. We prepare licence‑ready evidence and method statements and can support submissions to the relevant statutory body.

What guidance do you follow?

We align methods and reporting to current British Standards (e.g., BS 42020 and BS 42021) and industry best practice. Where project needs differ, we clearly justify any deviations.
