How the statutory pipeline runs

Clear checkpoints and responsibilities prevent last-minute scrambles.

Phase 1

Requirements mapping

We confirm regulator formats, licensing conditions, and planning obligations before processing begins.

Phase 2

Automated validation

Scripts test datasets against schema rules, coordinate systems, and QA tolerances with issue logs for resolution.

Phase 3

Formatting & review

Outputs are packaged with metadata summaries, version notes, and reviewer sign-off to evidence compliance.

Phase 4

Submission & aftercare

We upload, track feedback, and manage re-submissions—keeping clients informed via shared dashboards.

Frequently asked questions

Common queries from project managers and ecology leads.

How quickly can you turn around a BTO submission?

For pre-cleaned datasets we deliver exports within 2–3 working days. If QA flags major issues we’ll collaborate on remediation timelines so submissions still land before deadlines.

Can you work inside our existing SharePoint or Teams structure?

Yes. We configure automation and validation scripts to run within your existing Microsoft 365 environment, maintaining permissions and version histories.
