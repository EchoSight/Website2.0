How we build your mapping package

Structured collaboration keeps your data safe while we process and present it.

1. Intake & QC

We onboard field data, detector outputs, and consultant shapefiles through encrypted portals, documenting coordinate systems and source accuracy.

  • Source data catalogued with lineage and ownership
  • Projection checks and geometry validation on arrival
  • Gap analysis shared before processing begins

2. Analysis & styling

Our GIS specialists perform geoprocessing, calculate metrics, and apply corporate styling so your deliverables align with internal standards.

  • Automated geoprocessing scripts with manual review
  • Symbology libraries matched to your brand guidelines
  • Quality metrics reported for each derived layer

3. Review & approval

Draft maps are reviewed with your project leads using shared dashboards, ensuring annotations, buffers, and narratives match the survey evidence.

  • Shared dashboards with version comparisons
  • Commenting workflow for planners and ecologists
  • Automated changelog summarising updates

4. Delivery & support

Final packages include editable files, publication-ready PDFs, and guidance notes, with EchoSight on hand for planner or client queries.

  • Editable files plus locked publication exports
  • Guidance notes covering assumptions and usage
  • Optional handover calls for stakeholder teams

Integrations & governance

ArcGIS, QGIS & Power BI support

We deliver in the platforms you use most, including ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, Power BI, and SharePoint-hosted viewers.

  • Project templates configured for your environment
  • Model builder and Python tools supplied where required
  • Viewer links for stakeholders without desktop GIS

Live status trackers

Progress dashboards and shared change logs ensure everyone understands which layers are signed off and which are in review.

  • Real-time view of drafting, review, and approval states
  • Automatic notifications when tasks move stage
  • Downloadable reports summarising completed deliverables

GDPR-aware workflows

Sensitive data is segregated with access controls, and all deliverables include guidance on handling personal or protected site information.

  • Role-based access to datasets containing personal data
  • Redaction workflow for public-facing deliverables
  • Retention and deletion policies agreed upfront
