How we build your mapping package

Structured collaboration keeps your data safe while we process and present it.

1. Intake & QC

We onboard field data, detector outputs, and consultant shapefiles through encrypted portals, documenting coordinate systems and source accuracy.

2. Analysis & styling

Our GIS specialists perform geoprocessing, calculate metrics, and apply corporate styling so your deliverables align with internal standards.

3. Review & approval

Draft maps are reviewed with your project leads using shared dashboards, ensuring annotations, buffers, and narratives match the survey evidence.

4. Delivery & support

Final packages include editable files, publication-ready PDFs, and guidance notes, with EchoSight on hand for planner or client queries.
