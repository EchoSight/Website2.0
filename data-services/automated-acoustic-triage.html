How automated triage works

Automation handles the heavy lifting while experts make the final calls.

Stage 1

Model filtering

We deploy tuned machine learning classifiers to score call likelihood, remove noise, and categorise by species group.

Stage 2

Priority queueing

Calls above project-defined thresholds are routed to ecologists with contextual metadata and recommended actions.

Stage 3

Ecologist validation

Experienced analysts review flagged calls, annotate behaviours, and confirm identifications.

Stage 4

Reporting & feedback

Findings sync to dashboards, GIS layers, and reporting templates—feeding back into future model training.
