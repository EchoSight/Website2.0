What we manage

From day-to-day project control to final reporting packs.

Data governance
  • Permissions aligned to client and regulator requirements
  • Audit logs covering uploads, edits, and approvals
  • Retention policies for raw data, working files, and deliverables
Reporting
  • Dashboard updates summarising progress, risks, and milestones
  • Planning-ready reporting packs with appendices and evidence trails
  • Lessons-learned summaries to inform future seasons

Workflow overview

Clear stages that keep governance light-touch but effective.

Stage 1

Onboarding & permissions

We agree folder structures, naming conventions, and stakeholder access before intake begins.

Stage 2

Monitoring & QA

Dashboards track progress while QA checklists record reviews, ensuring traceability.

Stage 3

Reporting & approvals

Version-controlled reports, GIS outputs, and appendices are packaged for client and regulator sign-off.

Stage 4

Closeout

We archive data to agreed retention schedules and capture lessons for continuous improvement.
