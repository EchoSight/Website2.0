How the EchoSight Tracker workflow supports you

Designed to plug into your survey delivery with minimal friction.

1. Intake & alignment

Upload raw detector files and footage. EchoSight Tracker automatically time-aligns streams and flags key activity windows for ecologist review.

  • Encrypted upload links for large media packages
  • Automatic timestamp reconciliation across devices
  • Initial quality checks with feedback to field teams

2. Expert annotation

Licenced ecologists validate activity in the Tracker dashboard, capturing behaviour notes, species IDs, and quality checks.

  • Dual-review process for high-risk observations
  • Behaviour tags linked to mitigation guidance
  • Instant playback for client or planner queries

3. Reporting package

We deliver summary tables, charts, and annotated clips alongside editable text ready for planning documents.

  • Editable summary text aligned to survey templates
  • Charts and tables formatted for licence submissions
  • Downloadable clip library for stakeholder briefings
