From raw footage to actionable insight

Our structured process keeps reviewers aligned and evidence defensible.

Step 1

Footage intake

We catalogue camera deployments, angles, and timestamps, aligning them with site layouts and survey notes.

Step 2

Automated filtering

Motion detection and thermal triggers remove empty clips, focusing ecologists on relevant footage.

Step 3

Expert annotation

Specialists tag behaviours, species, and contextual notes in collaborative review platforms.

Step 4

Delivery & briefing

Clips, heatmaps, and summary tables are compiled for planning submissions and stakeholder briefings.
