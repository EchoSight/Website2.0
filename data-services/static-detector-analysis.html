How analysis is delivered

Transparent checkpoints keep you informed from data drop to final handover.

All species identification and reporting conclusions are made by a competent ecologist; automated tools are used solely to support detection, filtering, and data handling. EchoSight does not offer fully automated species identification or unsupervised AI-only analysis.

1. Secure intake

We ingest .wav, .zc, or Kaleidoscope projects via encrypted portals with metadata capture for detector IDs, locations, and deployment notes.

  • Automated intake receipts with unique dataset IDs
  • Weather and deployment notes stored alongside files
  • Immediate QA check for missing metadata

2. Pipeline triage

We process recordings using either the BTO acoustic analysis pipeline with proportionate QA or Anabat Insight using their methodology with ample species-level QA.

  • Detection and filtering to organise large datasets efficiently
  • Prioritisation of ambiguous or low-confidence calls for review
  • Clear routing for licence-sensitive species checks

3. Quality assurance

Experienced ecologists audit samples at scale, verify identifications, and document activity patterns with traceable QA notes.

  • Large-sample and targeted verification based on scale and risk
  • Review of ambiguous or low-confidence calls
  • Traceable, audit-ready decisions with reviewer commentary

4. Delivery & support

You receive spreadsheets, annotated call snippets, GIS exports, and recommendations for follow-up survey effort.

  • Species lists, activity charts, and GIS exports
  • Annotated call snippets packaged for evidence files
  • Follow-up advice for licensing or additional survey effort
