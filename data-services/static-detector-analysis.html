How the workflow runs

Transparent checkpoints keep you informed from data drop to final handover.

1. Secure intake

We ingest .wav, .zc, or Kaleidoscope projects via encrypted portals with metadata capture for detector IDs, locations, and deployment notes.

2. Smart triage

Machine learning filters and custom recogniser banks highlight likely target species while flagging uncertain calls for review.

3. Expert QA

Licenced ecologists audit the prioritised files, verify identifications, and document activity patterns with traceable QA notes.

4. Delivery & support

You receive spreadsheets, annotated call snippets, GIS exports, and recommendations for follow-up survey effort.
