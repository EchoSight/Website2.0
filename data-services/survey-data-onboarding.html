A workflow built for consistency

Four repeatable stages keep intake predictable, auditable, and fast.

Step 1

Secure transfer

We provide encrypted upload portals or manage SharePoint/Teams folders with permissions aligned to your governance.

Step 2

Intake validation

Automated scripts confirm naming conventions, file integrity, and metadata completeness before acceptance.

Step 3

QA review

Second-person checks reconcile deployment logs, survey notes, and file timestamps to flag anomalies early.

Step 4

Handover

Clean, catalogued datasets are surfaced in shared dashboards with task ownership assigned to processing leads.
