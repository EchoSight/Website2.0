Travel & accommodation (outside packages)

Survey packages are priced excluding travel. Travel is added from Newbury at £0.55/mi plus £38/hr travel time. Accommodation is added only when the site is more than 1.5 hours from Newbury (3-hour return trip).

  • Local travel (within 1.5 hours of Newbury): + travel only.
  • Over 1.5 hours from Newbury: + travel + client-funded accommodation (arranged by EchoSight if preferred).
  • Overnight surveys: subsistence can be agreed in advance for full-day or multi-day bookings.
