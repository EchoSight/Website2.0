Bat survey specialisms

Not every survey focuses on bats, but when it does you get targeted support that integrates licenced ecologists, live imaging, and end-to-end reporting.

Survey types
  • Preliminary Ecological Appraisal (PEA) and scoping assessments
  • Dusk and dawn activity surveys with real-time thermal imaging
  • Emergence and re-entry surveys tailored to building or tree roosts
  • Internal and external roost inspections with licenced ecologists
  • Remote static detectors (Batlogger, Anabat, Song Meter) for baseline monitoring
  • Natural England licence application and mitigation support
Deliverables
  • Methods and results aligned to BS 42020 and BS 42021
  • High-resolution mapping of activity and roost features
  • Risk assessment with clear recommendations and triggers
  • Mitigation and compensation outline ready for planning conditions
  • Appendices with call analysis, imagery, and raw data sets

Additional Information

Know exactly what’s included before we arrive on site.

  • All onsite packages include up to 60 minutes for equipment setup and clear-up as standard.
  • On-site survey time is exclusive of setup/clear-up—your 2 hours is all field time.
  • Travel up to 2 hours included (approx. 120 miles); additional mileage over 120 miles is charged at £0.45/mile.
  • Dual Deployment: EchoSight technician ensures on-site setup for both kits; analysis is post-survey only.
  • Dawn surveys carry a £40 surcharge per event.
  • Overnight stays required for sites over 90 minutes from Newbury; additional cost applies or accommodation can be client-booked.
  • Discounted bundles available for multi-phase or long-term projects.
  • All analysis assumes up to 2 hrs data per device per survey. Additional or complex data charged at £35/hr.
  • High-resolution thermal footage may require large file transfers; hard drive exchange can be organised.

You pay for accuracy, not corner cutting. Every survey and review is delivered to the highest professional standard with robust, repeatable, and transparent findings.
