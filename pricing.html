How we price projects

Rates reflect scope, access, survey windows, and reporting complexity. We’ll flag any third-party costs before you commit.

  • Scope: survey type, number of surveyors, and duration on site.
  • Access: travel time, site induction requirements, and H&S constraints.
  • Seasonality: peak survey windows may require rapid mobilisation or additional visits.
  • Reporting: level of detail, graphics, GIS outputs, and consultee requirements.

Travel & logistics

We base travel on our Newbury hub.

  • Projects within 60 miles: travel included.
  • Beyond 60 miles: £0.45 per mile plus overnight accommodation at cost if finish after 11pm.
  • Client-provided accommodation welcomed where available.

Additional costs

We’ll list any third-party charges in proposals.

  • Natural England licence fees billed directly to client.
  • Laboratory analysis (eDNA, histology) charged at supplier cost.
  • Specialist equipment hire (e.g., cherry picker, MEWP) recharged with receipts.
